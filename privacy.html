Privacy Policy

Aria & Coco creative tools, operated by Maria Lelkes, Berlin, Germany. Last updated 25 November 2026.

What this app is

This app is an internal tool. It lets Maria Lelkes create and manage advertising for her own animated series, Aria & Coco, in her own Meta ad account and on her own Facebook Page. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What data it uses

  • Access to the Aria & Coco ad account and Page, granted by the owner, to create campaigns, ad sets, ad creatives and ads.
  • Ad images and texts written for the series.
  • Aggregated ad performance figures from Meta, such as impressions, clicks and spend, to compare ad versions.

The app does not collect, store or process personal data about people who see or interact with the ads. It does not use Facebook Login for other users.

Where data is kept

Ad texts, image links, ad IDs and aggregated results are stored in a private spreadsheet that only the owner can access. Automations run on n8n.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties. It is used only to run and evaluate Aria & Coco advertising.

Data deletion

The app holds no personal data about ad viewers, so there is nothing to delete for them. To request deletion of any data related to you, or for any privacy question, contact Maria Lelkes via LinkedIn. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
